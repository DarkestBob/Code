```python
print("This is a band name generator program!")
city = input("Go ahead and input the city you grew up in! ")
pet = input("Now, input the name of your loveliest pet! ")
print("I suggest that your band name is: " + city + " " + pet)
```

Uses `input()` to collect two answers, then joins the city and pet name with `+` (string concatenation) to build the band name.
